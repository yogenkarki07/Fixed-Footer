Our Services

Buses offer the most extensive network coverage across the region, connecting residential suburbs directly to commercial hubs and local train stations. Standard services generally run every 15 to 30 minutes from 6:00 AM until midnight daily, while high-frequency routes arrive every 10 minutes during peak hours. Fares are calculated using a zone-based system, typically ranging from $2.50 for a single-zone journey to $5.50 for multi-zone travel when using a contactless transit card

Trains provide rapid, high-capacity transit along dedicated rail corridors, making them the most efficient option for bypassing peak-hour traffic congestion. Service schedules operate strictly from 5:30 AM to 11:30 PM, with frequencies hitting every 10 minutes during morning and evening commutes, and scaling back to 20 minutes off-peak. Single fares span between $3.00 and $7.00 depending on distance, with daily fare caps applied automatically to reward regular commuters.

Ferries deliver a scenic and efficient waterfront commute, seamlessly linking coastal communities and island destinations directly to the central city terminal. Due to maritime scheduling, sailings are highly structured, running every 20 to 30 minutes during morning and evening rush hours, and moving to hourly intervals during the middle of the day. Fares reflect the unique infrastructure required, costing between $5.00 and $12.00 per single trip, though substantial multi-trip discounts are available.
